# JSON Privacy Masker: Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-10-02 · **Draft: have it reviewed before submitting to a store.**

The same text is published on the website at `/privacy#extension` (use that URL in the store listing).

## What the extension collects

**Nothing.** JSON Privacy Masker does not collect, store, transmit or sell any data.

## How your text is handled

- When you right-click selected text and choose a JSON Privacy Masker option, your browser passes the selected text to the extension. The extension processes it **on your device** and shows the result in its popup.
- The selected text and the result are kept only in the extension's memory until the popup shows them, then cleared. They are never written to storage.
- Text you paste into the popup is processed the same way and disappears when the popup closes.
- The extension makes **no network requests**. Its content security policy blocks network connections (`connect-src 'none'`), and it loads no remote code.

## Permissions

The extension requests one permission, `contextMenus`, to add the right-click menu on selected text. It cannot read web pages on its own, and it has no access to your browsing history, tabs, cookies or other sites.

## Analytics and third parties

There are no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no third-party services in the extension.
