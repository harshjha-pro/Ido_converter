# Validation plan (14 days)

**Goal:** decide whether to invest further. The thresholds are experimental targets, not industry benchmarks.

1. **Days 1–2:** deploy; set the WhatsApp number; test on a real phone.
2. **Days 1–10:** contact 40–60 cataloguers (IndiaMART, Justdial, LinkedIn, Instagram) and seller groups (Facebook, Telegram). Messages are in the outreach drafts from earlier in the project.
3. **Interviews (15–20):** last time you prepared photos; volume per month; marketplaces; rejections; tools and spend; worst step; background removal needs; try the tool with your own files; price reaction; what you'd do if this disappeared.
4. **Price test:** show ₹199 / ₹399 / ₹599 and a cataloguer tier at ₹1,199.
5. **Commitment:** a refundable founding-user pre-payment (Razorpay link or UPI), or a paid done-for-you order.

| Signal | Proceed | Narrow | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recurring pain (≥ monthly rework) | ≥ 10 of 20 | 5–9 | < 3 |
| Batches with their own photos | ≥ 6 people | 3–5 | < 3 |
| Real payments | ≥ 3 | 1–2 | 0 after asking 15 |
| Unprompted return within 7 days | ≥ 2 | 1 | 0 |

**Data:** interview notes (yours), `validation_metrics.sql` (batches, pass rates, devices), and early-access leads.
