# Monetisation

_Status: hypotheses. Nothing is charged today. The site says "free during early access"._

| Layer | Who | What | Price to test | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free tools | Everyone | Everything that runs in the browser | ₹0 | Costs us almost nothing per use, so it's the acquisition channel |
| Done-for-you | Sellers with hard photos | We fix photos the tool can't (busy backgrounds) | ₹10–20 per photo or ₹499 per 50 (to test) | Earns from day one; tests willingness to pay |
| Credits (later) | Small sellers | Background removal | ~₹4–5 per image | Provider cost about ₹1.76 per image (Photoroom Basic, at the rate assumed earlier); keep ≥ 2–3× margin |
| Pro (later) | Cataloguers and agencies | Saved multi-client workflows, larger batches, credits included | ₹999–1,499/month | Highest willingness to pay among the segments compared |

**Costs today:** about ₹650/month (Hostinger, already paid). There are no per-use costs, since there's no AI.

**Unit economics, if a ₹399 plan is tested:** ₹338 after GST, about ₹14 payment fee → about ₹324 contribution before any AI costs.
